// Mission Systems

Everything the bike knows,
the way a rider needs it

F-43 Moto OS speaks directly to your ECU over Bluetooth OBD-II — no cloud, no account, no subscription. Data stays on your phone.

Live Cockpit

RPM, speed, gear, coolant, O2, fuel trims, load and more — eight gauge styles, from classic needle to the faceted HYPER dial, the fighter HUD ladder and the jet-turbine N1 — tuned for sunlight and night.

RIDE Mode

Above 8 km/h the cockpit morphs into a minimal HUD: big speed, lean angle, vitals, ride clock and phone battery — nothing you don't need at 80 km/h. On by default, yours to toggle.

PRO

Engine Wear Analysis

Eight continuous mechanic-grade checks — idle vacuum, O2 ageing, charging, wiring drop, air-filter breathing — reported in plain language.

PRO

Voice Navigation

Turn-by-turn OSM navigation with premium spoken guidance in your helmet — ducks your music, never cuts it.

Flight Debrief

Every ride becomes a shareable mission card — GPS trace, top speed, lean angle, economy — rendered in full cockpit style for your group chat.

Crash SOS

Detects a crash from motion + telemetry, counts down, then fires a pre-filled SOS text to your emergency contacts with your live location — one visible tap sends it (no hidden permissions). Always free.

Fuel & Maintenance

Jet fuel synoptic — quantity tape, live sloshing tank and a feed line to the engine — plus a fill-up log with real mileage, cost per 100 km, and chain & service reminders that follow your odometer.

Predictive Health

Watches every signal while you ride and warns early — weak charging, running lean, overheating, even an ECU that rebooted mid-ride — before they strand you.
